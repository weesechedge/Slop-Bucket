You must get through the entire intervening span before the potato farming unlocks. Until you are a potato farmer, you have not finished the game.

Rules, Stated Plainly

so there is no confusion later

The clock runs whether the tab is open or not. Time is real. That was the entire point. Closing the page does not pause the agricultural revolution, and neither does opening it.

Nothing you do affects the timer. Foraging, achievements, the microscope — none of it moves the 12,026 years. The tribe survives regardless of your input. You are not needed; you are only required to still be here at the end.

There is no fast-forward, no prestige, no idle multiplier, and no ending you can reach. The save is stored in this browser, on this device. If you clear it, you start the agricultural revolution again.
