    HOW TO PLAY ▾

    A famous quote is hidden. Every letter is a box; gaps between words and punctuation are shown for you. You get 5 guesses.

    Typing: just type. Letters flow across the boxes; press space to jump to the next word, Backspace to delete, click a box to move there. You can type a whole word, a single word in one spot, or the entire quote — then hit Enter.

    Colours (Wordle-style, but per word): right letter, right spot — locked in; that letter is somewhere in this word, wrong spot — held in place as a clue; not in this word.

    The twist: a yellow only tells you about the word you guessed it in. Finding an “e” in word 3 says nothing about “e” in any other word — every word keeps its own secrets.

    Each wrong guess unlocks one more clue about the quote. Solve every word to win.
