    HOW TO PLAY ▾

    A famous quote is hidden. The whole quote sits on one line of boxes — one box per letter, with spaces between words. You get 5 guesses.

    Each guess is a fresh line. Type your attempt at the quote — press space to jump to the next word, Backspace to delete, click a box to move there — then hit Enter. Your line is colour-graded and a new blank line appears below, so every guess stacks up and you can read each column to see what you’ve already nailed.

    Colours (Wordle-style, but graded per word): right letter, right spot; that letter is somewhere in this word, wrong spot; not in this word.

    The twist: a yellow only tells you about the word you guessed it in. Finding an “e” in word 3 says nothing about “e” in any other word — every word keeps its own secrets.

    Each wrong guess unlocks one more clue about the quote. Get the whole quote right on a single line to win — then copy your scorecard (one emoji per letter) and share it.
