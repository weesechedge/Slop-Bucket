The Combined Serviceyou are the conductor of the slow train, and you are also doing your actual job on the tray table

He said they were two different games. He also said they could be one, and that you should let people choose. So: you are working the Nordkapp–Lindesnes service as conductor, and you have brought the department with you. Both clocks run at once, in real time. The ministerial is due to the Office at 16:00. The train gets in at Lindesnes, which is a long way after that.

The two interfere, as they would. Tunnels drop the call — and there are a lot of tunnels between here and Trondheim. The announcement has to go out about five minutes before each stop, whatever else you are in the middle of. And you cannot take a meeting off mute in the quiet car.
