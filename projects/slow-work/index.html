Project · two real-time simulators, and both at once

Slow Worktwo games about the passing of actual time — a public sector day job, and a very long train

Both of these run at one second per second. Neither has a fast-forward, a speed multiplier or a skip, because the real-time part is not a constraint on the game — it is the game. Both keep running with the tab shut, so what you find when you come back is whatever had actually happened by then.

The brief was explicit that these were two different games, and then immediately less explicit: “But it could be. You let them choose. You know, maybe, mix and match.” So there are three doors.

7 h 36 m · one day A Day in the APS The full day job of a Commonwealth public servant, simulated in real time on their screen: the inbox that only goes up, meetings scheduled on top of meetings, the specific ways Teams fails, six unskippable minutes of mandatory learning, an assurance template that is returned and reissued at a higher version number, and a ministerial that has to clear eight desks before 16:00. Paste in your own assurance questions and complete them here, under these conditions. Work it, or just watchopen → 41 h 12 m · one run The Long Service You are the conductor on the through service from the top of Norway to the bottom — 2,900 km, thirty-three calls, Australian rolling stock. You pace up and down, check the occasional ticket, make the announcement five minutes out, and look out of the window at a landscape lit by your own local time. There are no emergencies.
