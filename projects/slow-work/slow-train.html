The Long Servicea very boring long ride from the top of Norway to the bottom, and you are the conductor

The through service from Nordkapp to Lindesnes: 2,900 kilometres, thirty-three calls, and 41 hours and 12 minutes — run at one second per second, on Australian rolling stock, which is why the buffet has a meat pie and a lamington. You are the conductor. Your duties are to pace up and down, check the occasional ticket, make the announcement about five minutes before each stop, answer the question about when we get in, and look out of the window.

Nothing happens. There are no emergencies. There is no attack in the steerage. The incident log is empty and will stay empty. The next stop is usually somewhere between forty minutes and an hour and a half away, and the train runs whether or not this tab is open — shut it overnight and you will wake up somewhere near Narvik.
