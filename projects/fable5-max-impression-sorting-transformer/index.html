Why this can’t be a lookup table: there are 50⁸ ≈ 39 trillion possible length-8 inputs and the model saw about 460,800 training examples — a few parts per hundred million of the space. Near-perfect accuracy on fresh lists means it learned a procedure, not answers.

What you’re running: token + positional embeddings → 2 pre-LN blocks of causal multi-head attention and a GELU MLP → unembed; greedy decoding. The same arithmetic as the NumPy training code, re-implemented in JavaScript, loading the exact trained weights (107,840 float32 values) embedded in this file. Every gradient used in training was derived by hand and verified against finite differences before the first step.
